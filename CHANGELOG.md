# Changelog

## v1.6.0 (2026-10-26)
- Resize one-shot text boxes vertically to read and edit longer content.
- Add named, clickable useful links at the top of campaigns and one-shots; links are saved with the adventure and included in backups.
- Ridimensiona verticalmente i box di testo delle one-shot per leggere e modificare i contenuti più lunghi.
- Aggiungi link utili con nome e indirizzo in cima a campagne e one-shot: i link sono salvati con l’avventura e inclusi nei backup.

## v1.5.0 (2026-10-26)
- Continue any one-shot as a sequel: heroes, monsters, locations, events, NPCs and items are cloned into a new adventure.
- The footer version button opens What is new with the changelog for each version.
- Continua una one-shot come seguito: eroi, mostri, luoghi, eventi, PNG e oggetti vengono clonati in una nuova avventura.
- Il pulsante della versione nel footer apre Novita con il registro delle modifiche per versione.

## v1.4.0 (2026-10-05)
- Bulk AI refinement for entire one-shot adventures.
- Read-only Ask buttons on one-shot sections.
- AI monster creation and guided content refinement.
- AI proxy with mobile usability improvements.

## v1.2.0 (2025-09-20)
- Fabula Ultima character sheets with PDF preview and download.
- Improved text wrapping in PDF generation.

## v1.1.0 (2025-09-09)
- One-shot adventure generation with NPC chat and premade templates.
- Battle maps with layers and dungeon types.
- Onboarding tutorial with GM and player modes.
- Fabula Ultima heroes with classes and multi-class support.

## v1.0.0 (2025-08-20)
- Campaigns with AI story continuation.
- Player mode with dice roller and NPC management.
- Hero and monster managers with backup and restore.
